OWASP Looks At An Application And Asks:

❓ Can Users Access Data They Shouldn't?

❓ Can Input Be Misused?

❓ Is Authentication Weak?

❓ Are Permissions Broken?

❓ Is Sensitive Data Exposed?

❓ Are Configurations Unsafe?

If Yes

Security Risk Exists.

OWASP Is Simply A Structured Way
Of Thinking About Application Risk.
